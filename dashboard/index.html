↓ How your downloads work

Why is there a download limit?

Download limits help protect the products you have paid for from unauthorized sharing, while still giving you reasonable, repeated access to your own purchase.

What happens if I reach my limit?

Your purchase is still yours. Reaching the limit does not mean you have lost access, it just means we need to verify the next request. Contact support and we will sort it out.

How do secure download links work?

Every download link is issued fresh and expires after a short window, for your security. If a link stops working, come back here and request a new one rather than reusing an old link.
