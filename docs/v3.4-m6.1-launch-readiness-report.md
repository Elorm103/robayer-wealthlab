## Critical issues

None found.

## Overall determination: Launch Ready

Every acceptance criterion in the brief was checked against real production execution, not code inspection alone. No Critical-severity issue was found. The items marked BLOCKED are architecture facts (features that do not exist yet, such as pre-purchase registration, dedicated media/partnership contact channels, and navigation/footer CMS editing), not defects in what does exist, and were already documented as known future work in M6's own Final Release Recommendation; nothing here changes that assessment or reveals a new one. The items marked PASS WITH OBSERVATIONS are genuine, lower-severity gaps worth addressing (most notably the static-asset cache-busting convention, which has a concrete recommended fix in the Infrastructure Verification Report) but none of them block real users from completing real workflows today.

## What would change this determination

If a real customer ever completes a genuine purchase with their own email address, the Customer Library Access and Review Eligibility items currently marked BLOCKED should be re-tested against that real account rather than continuing to rely on existing test-mode data, since that is the one workflow this review could not exercise with fully realistic data.
