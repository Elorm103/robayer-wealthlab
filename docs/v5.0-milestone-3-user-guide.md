# Customer AI User Guide — "Ask Robayer WealthLab"

**For: site visitors and customers**
**Feature: https://robayerwealthlab.com/ai-assistant/**

## What this is

A free assistant that answers your questions about saving, investing, and building wealth in Ghana, using Robayer WealthLab's own published guides and articles. Every answer is grounded in real content and shows you exactly which sources it used — nothing is invented.

## What it is not

It is an **educational guide, not a licensed financial advisor**. It will not:
- Recommend a specific stock, fund, or investment for you to buy
- Predict where the market or the Cedi is headed
- Give advice tailored to your personal income, age, savings, or goals

If you need guidance specific to your own situation, the assistant will point you to Robayer WealthLab's [consultation service](https://robayerwealthlab.com/consultation/).

## How to use it

1. Go to **[/ai-assistant/](https://robayerwealthlab.com/ai-assistant/)**, or click **"Ask AI"** in the site navigation.
2. Type your question in the box — e.g. "What are Treasury Bills?" or "How do I start budgeting with a small income?"
3. Press **Enter** to send (or **Shift+Enter** for a new line without sending).
4. The answer appears with:
   - A **confidence badge** (see below) telling you how well the Knowledge Base actually covers your question
   - A **"Show N sources"** link — click it to see and open the exact articles/guides the answer was built from
   - **Suggested follow-up questions** based on related content
   - **Copy** and **Regenerate** buttons, and **👍 Helpful / 👎 Not helpful** feedback buttons

## Understanding the confidence badge

| Badge | What it means |
|---|---|
| **High Confidence** | The source material is a strong, direct match for your question. |
| **Somewhat confident** | Relevant material exists, but it's not a perfect match — the answer will say so. |
| **Limited confidence** | Only partial information is available — the assistant will be upfront that the answer may be incomplete. |
| *(no answer, a decline message instead)* | Your question is outside what the Knowledge Base currently covers. Rather than guess, the assistant will say so honestly and suggest browsing the site directly. |

## Conversation and privacy

- Your conversation lives only in your current browser tab. Nothing is tied to your name, email, or account.
- Asking a follow-up question naturally reuses your last few questions and answers so the assistant understands context — this never leaves your browser except as part of your very next question.
- Click **"Reset conversation"** at any time to start fresh and clear everything.
- Closing the tab clears your conversation permanently. Nothing is saved for next time.

## Rate limits

To keep the service fair and available to everyone, you can ask up to **15 questions every 5 minutes**. If you hit this limit, you'll see a message asking you to wait a few minutes.

## If something goes wrong

If the assistant shows an error, click **Retry**. If it keeps happening, the site's usual [Contact](https://robayerwealthlab.com/contact/) page is always available as a fallback.
