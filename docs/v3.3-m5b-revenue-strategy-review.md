# Version 3.3 Sprint M5B: Revenue Strategy Review

Independently reviews every revenue opportunity M5A's Revenue Opportunity Report identified, evaluating short term impact, long term impact, implementation complexity, and expected return, and checks for any missed opportunity.

---

## Review of Identified Opportunities

| Opportunity | Short Term Impact | Long Term Impact | Implementation Complexity | Expected Return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Activate coupon and newsletter systems with a real campaign | Low absolute (15 subscribers), but immediate | Establishes a repeatable campaign cadence for future catalog growth | None (both systems already built, independently confirmed present and functional) | High relative to cost, since the cost is close to zero |
| Reconcile orphaned guest purchases into accounts | Low direct revenue, high indirect (unlocks reviews, which support future conversion through social proof) | High: converts a closed customer relationship into an open one for 15 of 17 lifetime customers | Medium (confirmed technically viable for all fifteen cases; see below) | Favorable, though largely a trust and retention return rather than a direct revenue one |
| Visitor and marketing analytics | None directly | High: every future revenue decision becomes evidence-based rather than a guess | Low (native Cloudflare integration, independently confirmed to require no backend change) | Very high relative to cost |
| Second product launch | High, once shipped | High: unlocks every catalog-dependent revenue lever at once | Not an engineering item; a content and business timeline decision | High, but outside this milestone's control |

This Board's independent technical check on the account-reconciliation opportunity specifically: all fifteen orphaned purchases carry a real, non-empty, payment-provider-verified `customer_email` (never a client-supplied value, confirmed by schema comment), and represent fifteen distinct people with zero overlap against the two existing customer accounts. This means the opportunity is fully realizable, not merely mostly realizable, a stronger confirmation than M5A's own report provided.

## Missed Revenue Opportunities

This Board's own independent review, conducted without assuming M5A's list was complete, did not surface a materially different revenue opportunity than the four already identified. One refinement worth naming explicitly: M5A's report treats "promotional campaigns" as a single opportunity; this Board notes that the coupon engine's own `first_purchase_only` flag (built in Milestone M4, confirmed present in the schema) makes a first-purchase-specific discount a distinct, ready-to-use variant of the general campaign opportunity, worth calling out to whoever executes the campaign M5A already recommends as an immediate business action.

## Verdict

**No amendment required.** M5A's revenue strategy is sound, its highest-ranked opportunities are correctly ordered, and this Board's own independent review did not find a higher-value opportunity M5A overlooked. The one refinement above (the `first_purchase_only` coupon flag as a ready-to-use campaign variant) is a helpful addition, not a correction.
