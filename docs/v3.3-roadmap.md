# Version 3.3 Sprint M5A: Roadmap

Recommends M5, M6, and M7, and explains the sequencing logic behind each.

---

## M5: Activation, Analytics, and Customer Reconciliation

See `v3.3-m5-recommended-scope.md` for the full scope. Three items: legacy guest-purchase account reconciliation, visitor and marketing analytics, and a triggered post-purchase review-request email.

**Why first:** every other candidate milestone depends, directly or indirectly, on data or reach that M5 provides. A second product's catalog-expansion milestone (M6, below) is better planned with real conversion and traffic data in hand. A future marketplace milestone (M7, below) is better justified with a larger, better-measured, better-retained single-seller customer base behind it first. M5 is also the only candidate milestone that repairs an existing gap (the orphaned guest purchases) rather than adding new surface area, and that gap only grows if left for a later milestone.

## M6: Catalog Expansion and Lifecycle Marketing

Recommended scope, contingent on the second product ("The MoMo Savings Playbook") actually shipping as content: multi-product catalog support for upsells, cross-sells, and bundles (all currently blocked by the single-product catalog), a real promotional campaign cadence using the now-activated coupon and newsletter systems, and lifecycle email sequences built using the real conversion and retention data M5's analytics will have produced by then.

**Why second, not first:** every feature in this milestone requires either a second live product or real usage data neither of which exists yet. Sequencing it after M5 means it is built against evidence, not assumption, the same discipline this project's own Milestone M4B review insisted on when it independently re-verified Milestone M4A's own claims rather than accepting them.

## M7: Marketplace and Platform Monetization

Recommended scope, contingent on a genuine business decision to pursue a multi-seller or creator marketplace model: the `platform_users` schema rename this project's own ADR-011 has already gated behind exactly this milestone, a real Platform Fee Engine (see `v3.3-platform-fee-engine-assessment.md` for why it only makes sense once this milestone's own precondition, multiple sellers, exists), an affiliate program, and a fresh evaluation of course-platform demand using whatever real customer signal has accumulated by then.

**Why third, and why long-term rather than near-term:** this is the same designation this project's own prior planning history (Milestone M4A, Milestone M4B) already assigned to this cluster of features, for the same reason repeated in every review of it since: zero current demand signal, and a business-model change (multiple sellers, revenue splitting) significant enough that building it speculatively ahead of demand would repeat exactly the kind of premature-design mistake Milestone M4B's own independent review caught once already (the original coupon schema, designed for a marketplace that did not exist).

---

## Sequencing Summary

M5 produces the data and repairs the customer-base gap that M6 needs to be evidence-based rather than assumption-based. M6 produces the multi-product, better-measured customer base that would make an eventual M7 marketplace decision a genuinely informed one, rather than a speculative one. Each milestone's precondition is the previous milestone's output, not a parallel or independent track.
