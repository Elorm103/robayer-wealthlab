# Version 3.3 Sprint M5B: Roadmap Validation

Independently reviews M5A's proposed M5, M6, and M7 sequencing, and recommends changes only where supported by clear evidence.

---

## M5: Activation, Analytics, and Customer Reconciliation

Independently confirmed as the correct first milestone. Every precondition-based argument M5A makes (M6 needs real conversion data, M7 needs a larger and better-measured customer base first) holds up under this Board's own scrutiny, and this Board's own additional evidence (the precise, provider-verified nature of the orphaned-purchase data, the confirmed zero-repeat-purchase history across the entire platform, not only among account holders) further supports sequencing this milestone first rather than weakening the case for it.

## M6: Catalog Expansion and Lifecycle Marketing

Independently confirmed as correctly sequenced second, contingent on the second product actually shipping as content, a dependency this Board agrees is a business and content timeline question outside engineering's own control to accelerate. One addition this Board recommends for M6's own eventual scope, not a change to its sequencing: revisit the Checkout Service Fee Engine (not the Marketplace Commission Engine) at this point, using M5's own analytics data, if and only if a concrete business reason has emerged by then (see `v3.3-m5b-platform-fee-strategy-review.md`). This is an addition to M6's candidate list, not a requirement.

## M7: Marketplace and Platform Monetization

Independently confirmed as correctly deferred to long term, matching this project's own pre-existing ADR-011 designation, independently re-located and re-confirmed by this Board rather than accepted from M5A's own citation of it. The Marketplace Commission Engine specifically (as distinct from the Checkout Service Fee Engine) belongs here and nowhere earlier, since it has no meaning without the multi-seller precondition this milestone itself would establish.

---

## Changes Recommended

**One:** M6's own candidate list should explicitly include a data-driven Checkout Service Fee Engine re-evaluation, distinct from the Marketplace Commission Engine question, which correctly remains at M7. This is an addition, not a reordering.

**No other change recommended.** This Board's independent review of the roadmap's own sequencing logic (each milestone's precondition being the previous milestone's output) found the logic sound and did not find evidence supporting a different order.

## Verdict

**Roadmap approved as sequenced, with one addition to M6's candidate list.** No milestone should be reordered, accelerated, or removed based on the evidence this Board independently gathered.
