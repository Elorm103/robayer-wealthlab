Consolidated, prioritized recommendations for Sprint M5C, derived from every deliverable in this review.

## Must Complete Before M5C Implementation Begins

1. **Produce the separated Checkout Service Fee Engine versus Marketplace Commission Engine documentation**, using the distinction and conclusions in `v3.3-m5b-platform-fee-strategy-review.md`, either as an update to `v3.3-platform-fee-engine-assessment.md` or as a standalone correction referencing it. ## Should Be Reflected in M5C's Own Implementation Design

2. **Document the two-step account-reconciliation sequence explicitly** (create-or-find a customer record, then issue a password-setup token), so the implementation team does not assume the existing `customer_password_tokens` mechanism alone covers a person with no customer row yet.
3. **Build the review-request Cron Trigger idempotently from the start**, matching this project's own established webhook-idempotency discipline, and test this specifically, not merely assume it from precedent.
4. **Keep the reconciliation flow's trust boundary identical to the existing password-reset flow**: a token sent to the payment-provider-verified email on file, never a claim accepted on the strength of a typed-in email alone.

## Track for M6 (Not M5C's Responsibility)

7. Add a data-driven Checkout Service Fee Engine re-evaluation to M6's own candidate list, contingent on a concrete business reason emerging from M5's own analytics data. Not a requirement; a tracked candidate.

## Process Recommendation

This Board's own independent re-querying of every production figure M5A cited found zero discrepancies, a genuinely strong result. The one reasoning gap this review did find (the blended Platform Fee Engine treatment) occurred specifically in the one mandatory assessment M5A's own brief asked it to treat as a distinct, separately-reasoned evaluation and did not fully do so. Future planning cycles asked to evaluate two related but distinct business capabilities in one document should structure that document with two clearly separated sections from the start, exactly the discipline this Board applied in producing `v3.3-m5b-platform-fee-strategy-review.md`.
