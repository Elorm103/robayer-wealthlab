## Known Limitations

Documented honestly, not speculatively:

1. **21 pre-existing test failures** in the checkout/webhook/coupon integration suite, confirmed unrelated to this milestone's changes, tracked as a separate background item.
2. **`getEmailLifecycleSummary` is not classification-aware** — by design, since `email_log` carries no `data_classification` column (operational history, not a business record).
3. **The Archive Centre's relation map is not exhaustive** — it covers the FK relationships relevant to records this project has actually left `UNKNOWN`, not a full 22×22 relationship graph.
4. **No formal Launch Baseline snapshot has been captured yet** through the immutable `production_launch_baselines` table — the figures above are live-computed, not yet frozen into that table. Capturing the first official entry is a founder action, not performed automatically.
5. **Zero Production-tier customers or revenue exist as of this certificate** — this is the honest, correct state of a platform that has completed its technical build but has not yet made its first arms-length sale. This is a distribution/marketing milestone, not a technical defect.
6. **Local development D1 is stale** relative to the current production schema (missing several columns added over the platform's history) — a pre-existing condition unrelated to this session, worth a future cleanup pass before it affects local testing.

No other known issues exist. No speculative or hypothetical concerns are listed here.

## Version 4 Series — Engineering Achievements

A permanent record of what the Version 4 series built, from Version 4.0 through this 4.9 certification:

- **CMS maturity**: full editorial control over products, resources, blog posts, and media — with metadata completeness checks, broken-reference detection, and a proper media library with duplicate-detection hashing.
- **Executive Dashboard**: a real, from-scratch business intelligence surface — revenue, orders, conversion, customer insights, sales charts, operational feeds, business alerts, and traffic/funnel analytics — built entirely on live D1 aggregates, with an explicit "no placeholder metrics" discipline throughout.
- **Analytics**: a real, anonymous page-view/CTA-click/traffic-source measurement layer (`analytics_events`), plus this release's full classification-aware separation of every dashboard metric between genuine business activity and internal/test data.
- **Customer Accounts**: customer identity, sessions, and profile storage, built on the same proven session/CSRF pattern already established for admins.
- **Purchase Lifecycle**: checkout → Paystack verification → order artifacts (order items, licenses, receipts, deliveries) → entitlement-gated downloads, with coupon support and race-condition-safe redemption limits.
- **Email Lifecycle**: a full customer-journey email system (newsletter welcome, free-guide delivery, purchase receipt, secure download, follow-up, review reminder, campaign sends) with per-template diagnostics and delivery tracking.
- **Product Bundles**: additive, non-breaking bundle support (`products.is_bundle` + `bundle_items`) layered onto the existing product model without restructuring it.
- **Archive Centre** *(this release)*: filter-based, non-destructive visibility into every classified business table, with an evidence-and-relations review workflow for ambiguous records.
- **Production Classification** *(this release)*: an explicit, never-guessed four-tier model (PRODUCTION/INTERNAL/DEVELOPMENT/UNKNOWN) applied across 22 tables with a full evidence trail, replacing an implicit assumption that all data was equally "real."
- **Launch Baseline** *(this release)*: an append-only, database-trigger-enforced immutable record of true launch metrics — proven immutable by direct experiment, not merely documented as such.
- **Security**: session/CSRF discipline held consistently across every new admin surface this release added; secrets never touch application code or version control; every classification change and analytics-mode preference change is audit-logged through the platform's one existing audit mechanism, never a parallel one.
- **Performance**: no new caching layers or pre-aggregation tables were introduced where real row counts don't yet justify them — a deliberate, stated engineering choice carried consistently from `analyticsService.ts` through to this release's Archive Centre and Launch Baseline services.
