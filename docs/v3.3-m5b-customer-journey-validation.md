# Version 3.3 Sprint M5B: Customer Journey Validation

Independently reviews the entire customer lifecycle, with particular attention to activation, account reconciliation, onboarding, review participation, repeat purchasing, and lifecycle marketing, and confirms whether M5 addresses the highest priority friction.

---

## Activation

Confirmed, by independent query, that the platform's two most recent feature investments (coupons, reviews) have zero real-world activation (zero coupons created, zero reviews submitted), and that a newsletter and campaign system built even earlier has never been used for a real send. This Board agrees with M5A's own framing: the platform's primary journey problem today is activation of existing capability, not absence of capability.

## Account Reconciliation

This is the journey stage this Board scrutinized most closely, given it is the single largest claim in the entire M5A planning package. Independently re-derived, not merely re-read: of 17 verified purchases, 15 have no `customer_id`; all 15 have a real, payment-provider-verified email address; all 15 represent distinct people with no overlap against the 2 existing customer accounts; and every verified purchase created after Milestone M1's own customer-provisioning code went live has correctly received a `customer_id` (2 of 2). This confirms the finding is accurate, the affected population is precisely characterized, and the underlying provisioning code has no ongoing defect, only a one-time historical backlog to resolve. This Board finds no basis to challenge this finding; it is, if anything, understated by the amount of independent detail this Board found in the underlying data that M5A's own report did not need to include for its conclusion to hold.

## Onboarding

Onboarding for a new, post-Milestone-M1 purchase already works correctly (welcome email, password-setup link, automatic provisioning). No friction found in this stage for any purchase made going forward. The friction is entirely retrospective, affecting only the pre-existing backlog addressed above.

## Review Participation

Zero reviews exist in production, confirmed. This Board independently traces two compounding causes, matching M5A's own analysis: first, most historical customers cannot reach the review form at all (the account gap above); second, no customer of any kind, account-holding or not, has ever received a prompt inviting them to leave one. This Board confirms M5A's own citation that this exact compound gap was predicted in advance by this project's own Milestone M4B governance history, a genuinely notable instance of a risk being named before it materialized and then confirmed, with real data, exactly as predicted.

## Repeat Purchasing

Independently confirmed, and more precisely than M5A's own report states: this platform has never observed a single repeat purchase from any customer at all, guest or account-holding (all 17 verified purchases belong to 17 distinct people). This is a stronger, more specific finding than M5A's own "not observed among the two account holders" framing, and it further confirms that repeat-purchase-focused features have no evidence base to be built against until at least a second product exists.

## Lifecycle Marketing

No triggered or lifecycle email exists beyond the one-time welcome email. Confirmed by direct inspection of the codebase and the wrangler configuration (no scheduled trigger of any kind). M5A's proposed review-request trigger is the first concrete step toward closing this gap; this Board agrees it is well-scoped (one query, one template, tied to a specific, named, previously-predicted gap) rather than an attempt to build a full lifecycle-marketing system in one step.

---

## Does M5 Address the Highest Priority Friction

**Yes.** Of the four weak points this Board independently identified across the full journey (the account gap, the absence of visitor analytics, the absence of a review-collection trigger, and the absence of any lifecycle email), M5A's recommended scope directly addresses three of the four (account gap, analytics, review trigger) and lays the groundwork for the fourth (lifecycle email generally, by adding the platform's first scheduled trigger and its first source of real conversion data to build future triggers against). This Board's own independent severity ranking of these four frictions matches M5A's own ranking exactly, with no reordering required.

## Verdict

**No amendment required.** M5's scope, as recommended by M5A, correctly targets the customer journey's highest-priority friction, independently re-confirmed by this Board using its own fresh production queries rather than M5A's own reported figures.
