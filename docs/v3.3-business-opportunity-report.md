# Version 3.3 Sprint M5A: Business Opportunity Report

Reviews revenue opportunities, customer trust, marketing capabilities, retention, acquisition, and support costs, with an estimate of business impact for each, grounded in the real production evidence documented in `v3.3-product-review.md`.

---

## Revenue Opportunities

Covered in full in `v3.3-revenue-opportunity-report.md`. Summary: the two highest-return actions available (activating the existing coupon and newsletter systems, and reconciling orphaned guest purchases into accounts) both require modest or zero new engineering, since the underlying systems already exist. Estimated business impact: low absolute revenue in the near term (the current customer base is small), but meaningfully higher relative to doing nothing, at near-zero incremental cost.

## Customer Trust

Already strong, and a genuine asset: honest empty-state copy, no manufactured urgency, licensed-institution-only guidance, an explicit scam-warning chapter in the flagship product, a real refund policy. The account-reconciliation gap (15 of 17 customers unable to access their own purchase history through a dashboard) is a trust-relevant weakness worth naming here specifically, not only as a technical or journey issue: a customer who paid real money and has no way to log in and see what they bought is a trust gap, even though nothing was ever technically broken for them. Estimated business impact of closing it: meaningful, since trust and word-of-mouth are likely this early-stage business's main acquisition channel (no analytics exists to confirm this, which is itself the analytics gap's own cost).

## Marketing Capabilities

A newsletter system with 15 subscribers and a coupon engine both exist and have never been used together. This is a real, underused marketing capability, not a gap requiring new engineering. Estimated business impact of a single real campaign: modest in absolute terms given the subscriber count, but the marginal cost is close to zero, making the expected return favorable regardless of scale.

## Retention

No formal retention mechanic exists. At 2 account-holding customers, this is appropriately unbuilt; the estimated business impact of building retention tooling now, before the account base is larger, is low, since there is not yet a meaningful base to retain.

## Acquisition

Cannot be assessed with confidence: no visitor analytics exists to show where the 2 existing customers, 15 newsletter subscribers, or 1 consultation request came from. This is the report's clearest finding: **the business cannot currently measure its own acquisition channels at all.** Estimated business impact of closing this gap: high, since every other acquisition decision (content strategy, paid promotion, partnership) is currently made without the data to evaluate it.

## Support Costs

Low today (0 contact messages, 1 consultation request in the platform's entire history), and the platform's existing self-service surfaces (guest receipt download, dashboard, FAQ content) likely account for this. No evidence in this review suggests support costs are a near-term business concern; no change recommended in this area for Version 3.3.

---

## Overall Business Impact Estimate

The platform's business opportunity is not currently constrained by missing features. It is constrained by under-activated existing capability (coupons, newsletter, reviews) and by a measurement gap (no visitor analytics) that makes every other business decision harder to evaluate correctly. The highest-leverage Version 3.3 investment, from a pure business-impact standpoint, is closing the measurement gap and the account-reconciliation gap, both of which unlock the value of work this business has already paid for (Milestone M4's coupon and review engines) rather than asking it to pay for new work before using what it already built.
