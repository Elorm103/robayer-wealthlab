# Version 3.2 - Sprint M4B - Risk Review

Independently reviews business, engineering, delivery, and operational risk for the proposed M4 scope, and answers the sprint's own explicit question: is this scope realistically achievable in a single milestone?

---

## Business Risk

M4A's Business Risk table is independently confirmed accurate (VAT escalation credibility, governance-amendment risk, premature-course-platform-pressure risk). One addition from this Board's own findings: **the coupon-schema correction itself carries a small business risk if missed** - if M4C implementation proceeded using M4A's cited schema literally, it would fail at migration time (referencing a nonexistent table), a wasted-effort risk now fully mitigated by this review having caught it before implementation began.

## Engineering Risk

**Elevated from M4A's own characterization**, for the reason detailed in the Architecture Readiness Assessment: Coupons touches the payment-amount-locking/webhook-verification calculation, the most safety-critical code path in the system. This Board rates this **Medium**, not M4A's implicit Low-Medium - not because the change is unsafe (the existing pattern extends cleanly), but because this is exactly the class of change (a modification to already-hardened, previously-incident-adjacent logic) that this project's own history shows deserves the most scrutiny, not the least. M4C's Security Review should treat this as its primary focus area, not one item among several.

**Confirmed Low, unchanged from M4A:** Reviews carries no comparable risk - it is a pure, additive, ownership-gated feature with no interaction with payment logic.

## Delivery Risk

**Is the proposed scope realistically achievable in a single milestone?** This Board's assessment: **yes, with the schema correction folded in as the first step of M4C rather than treated as separately blocking.** M4A's own complexity estimate ("comparable to M3") remains reasonable once the schema-correction work (small - this Board estimates it as a partial-sprint task, not a sprint of its own) is accounted for. The Board does not recommend splitting M4 into two milestones over this finding - the correction is real but contained.

## Operational Risk

No change from M4A's own assessment - M4 introduces no new operational surface (no scheduled jobs, no new infrastructure category). Confirmed by this Board's own check of `wrangler.jsonc` (still zero `triggers.crons` entries, unaffected by M4's scope).

---

## Verdict on Single-Milestone Achievability

**Achievable**, on the condition that the coupon-schema correction is completed early in M4C (or as a short technical addendum immediately preceding it) rather than discovered mid-implementation the way this project's own history shows unaddressed gaps tend to surface (at the acceptance-review stage, the more expensive place to find them). This is the single actionable difference between this Board's risk posture and M4A's own.
