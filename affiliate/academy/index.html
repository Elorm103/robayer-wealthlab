Module 1

Welcome

Robayer WealthLab publishes practical, Ghana-focused financial education — books, guides, and courses that help people understand money, savings, investing, and financial planning in terms that actually apply to their lives here. The Affiliate Programme exists because the best way most people find this content is a recommendation from someone they already trust: you.

As an affiliate, your job isn't to sell. It's to point people who are already curious about a topic — treasury bills, budgeting, mobile money savings — toward a resource that will actually help them, and be honestly rewarded when that recommendation leads to a sale. This Academy walks through how that works in practice, what's expected of you, and how to do it well.

Module 2

Understanding Your Role

An affiliate is not an employee, a reseller, or a spokesperson for Robayer WealthLab. You don't set prices, you can't offer discounts we haven't published, and you don't speak for us in any official capacity. What you are is an independent recommender: someone who shares a link, and earns a commission when that link leads to a genuine purchase.

That distinction matters for how you present yourself. Say "I recommend this book" or "I've found this useful," not "we" when talking about Robayer WealthLab, and don't imply an employment or partnership relationship beyond being an approved affiliate. Clarity here protects both your audience's trust and your own credibility.

Module 3

Our Products

Before recommending anything, know what it actually covers. Skim the product page, read the description, and where possible read or skim the content itself — a recommendation built on a real understanding of the material is more convincing, more honest, and far less likely to mislead a buyer about what they're getting.

Products vary in depth and audience: some are short, practical guides aimed at beginners; others go deeper into specific strategies. Match the recommendation to the person — a total beginner to saving doesn't need the same resource as someone already investing. The marketing resources page lists current products with descriptions and approved assets to help with this.

Module 4

Affiliate Links & Tracking

Every approved affiliate gets a unique referral code, built into a link on your Referral Links page. When someone clicks it, a first-party cookie records that visit against your code for 30 days. If they buy within that window, using the same browser they clicked in, the sale is attributed to you and a commission is calculated automatically.

A few things worth understanding about how this works: if someone clicks more than one affiliate's link before buying, the most recent click gets credit — so timing matters more than being first. The tracking is entirely server-side, so it can't be spoofed or faked from your end, and it also can't be recovered if a visitor clears cookies or switches devices between clicking and buying — there's no way around that, so the honest strategy is simply to make the link easy to click right when someone's ready to act.

Never share your link is a request that people click it without any genuine interest, and never use paid traffic tactics (like ad platforms) that violate those platforms' own policies. See Module 7 for what counts as acceptable versus prohibited promotion.

Module 5

Creating Useful Content

The content that converts best is the content that's actually useful on its own, with or without a purchase at the end of it. A short write-up of "three things I learned about treasury bills" that happens to link to the book people can read for more, works far better than a bare "buy this" post.

Some formats that work well: a personal story about a financial decision you made and what you wish you'd known sooner; a summary of one useful idea from a book, credited honestly, with a link to read the rest; a comparison of a few approaches to a common problem (e.g. "three ways Ghanaians save for irregular income"), where a Robayer WealthLab resource is one relevant option among your points, not the entire post. Quality and honesty consistently outperform volume.

Module 6

Social Media Promotion

Social platforms are a natural fit for affiliate sharing, but each has norms worth respecting. Disclose that a link is an affiliate link where the platform expects it (a simple "affiliate link" or "#ad" is usually enough) — this isn't just good practice, it's often a platform or regulatory requirement, and it protects your credibility with your own audience.

Avoid link-only posts with no context, avoid posting the same link repeatedly in a short window (this reads as spam and often gets suppressed by the platform itself), and never buy followers, use bot engagement, or run coordinated fake-engagement campaigns to inflate a post's reach. If you run paid ads on a platform, that platform's own advertising policies and disclosure rules apply in full, in addition to Robayer WealthLab's own affiliate terms.

Module 7

Ethical & Responsible Promotion

This is the module most worth taking seriously. Financial products and education carry real consequences for the people who act on them, so the bar for honesty is higher here than for most affiliate categories.

Never claim guaranteed returns, guaranteed outcomes, or "get rich" results. Robayer WealthLab's content teaches principles and strategies — it doesn't guarantee any specific financial result, and neither should you. Don't misrepresent what a product contains (e.g. claiming a beginner guide covers advanced trading strategies it doesn't). Don't impersonate Robayer WealthLab, its founder, or staff, and don't create fake urgency ("only 2 left," "price doubles tomorrow") that isn't actually true.

Self-referrals — buying through your own link, or having someone close to you do it on your behalf to generate a commission — are not allowed and are checked for automatically; any commission from a self-referral is void. Spam (unsolicited bulk messages, comment-flooding, mass DMs) is not allowed under any circumstance.

Module 8

Understanding Commissions

Your commission rate is shown on your dashboard once approved, and applies to the sale price of eligible purchases made through your tracked link within the 30-day attribution window described in Module 4. Commissions are recorded when a qualifying purchase completes and moves through pending, payable, and paid stages as it clears — your Earnings page shows exactly where each one stands.

A commission can be reversed if the underlying purchase is refunded or found to be fraudulent, or if it's later found to be a self-referral. Payouts are requested from your Earnings page once your payable balance clears the minimum threshold shown there.

Module 9

Your Affiliate Dashboard

Once approved, your Overview page shows clicks, conversions, conversion rate, and revenue generated at a glance. Referral Links is where you get your trackable link for any product. Earnings breaks down every commission by status and lets you request a payout. Resources has approved marketing assets and product descriptions ready to use.

Check in periodically rather than constantly — clicks and conversions update as real visits and purchases happen, so there's nothing to refresh in real time, but tracking what content or channel is actually converting for you is the fastest way to improve your results over time.

Module 10

Affiliate Code of Conduct

In short, as an approved Robayer WealthLab affiliate you agree to:

  • Represent products accurately, based on real familiarity with what they contain.
  • Never guarantee financial outcomes or returns.
  • Disclose affiliate links where your audience or the platform expects it.
  • Never self-refer or arrange for someone else to buy through your link on your behalf.
  • Never spam, use bot traffic, or fake engagement.
  • Never impersonate Robayer WealthLab or misrepresent your relationship to it.
  • Follow the terms and policies of any platform you promote on.

Violating this code can result in suspension or termination from the programme, and forfeiture of any commission tied to the violation. The full legal terms are in the Affiliate Programme Terms; this module is the practical summary.
