How referral links work

  • When someone clicks your link, we remember it's you for 30 days.
  • If they buy anything within that window, you earn commission, even if they browse other pages first.
  • If someone clicks a different affiliate's link afterward, the most recent link wins.
  • Purchases made with your own account never earn commission.
