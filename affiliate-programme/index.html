Affiliate Programme Terms

Commission

Your commission rate is set when your application is approved and shown in your dashboard. It's calculated on the actual amount a customer pays (after any coupon discount), never on the original list price.

What counts as a qualifying sale

A sale qualifies when a customer completes and pays for an order within 30 days of clicking your referral link, using the same browser they clicked it in. If a customer clicks more than one affiliate's link, the most recent click gets credit. Purchases made with your own account, or an account you control, never qualify.

When commission becomes payable

A new commission starts as Pending, then moves to Approved and Payable as our team reviews it; there is no fixed, automatic timeframe for this. You can request a payout for your full payable balance once it reaches the minimum threshold shown in your dashboard.

Refunds and cancellations

If an order tied to your commission is refunded or cancelled before payout, that commission is reversed and you'll see the reason in your dashboard. A commission that has already been paid out is never automatically clawed back.

Self-referrals and prohibited practices

You may not earn commission on your own purchases. We don't allow spam, misleading claims about Robayer WealthLab or its products, impersonation, or artificial/bot-generated clicks. We may suspend or terminate an affiliate account for any of these, or for other abuse of the programme.

Payouts

Payouts are reviewed and processed by our team; there is no guaranteed processing time. We may ask you to confirm payout details before processing.

Changes and termination

We may update these terms, adjust commission structures for future sales, or suspend/terminate the programme at our discretion. Commission already earned under the terms in effect at the time of a sale is not retroactively changed.

This summary is written in plain business language for clarity. It is not a substitute for formal legal terms and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before the programme scales significantly.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a cost to join?

No. The Affiliate Programme is free to join.

How much can I earn?

Your commission rate is shown in your dashboard once approved. We don't make income guarantees or projections: how much you earn depends entirely on the sales you genuinely help create.

Do I need a large following?

No. Approval is based on the quality and legitimacy of your application, not follower counts.

Can I recruit other affiliates for a cut of their earnings?

No. The programme has no multi-level or recruitment structure. You only ever earn from sales you personally refer.
